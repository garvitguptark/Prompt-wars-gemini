      Smog Myths, Checked

      Ask AirWise ↗

      • Myth

        Indoor plants clean smoggy air

        Not measurably. A HEPA purifier or a box-fan filter does.

      • Myth

        A surgical mask is enough

        It does little against PM2.5. A well-fitted N95 filters at least 95%.

      • Myth

        Fresh air always helps

        Only in the day's cleanest hours. Otherwise keep the windows shut.

      • Myth

        Smog only comes from outside

        Incense, mosquito coils and frying are big indoor sources.

      • Myth

        Purifiers work with windows open

        They can't keep up. Shut the room and run it on high.
